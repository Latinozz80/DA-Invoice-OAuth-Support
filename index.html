DA Invoice Automation V4

A private invoice preparation tool operated by Daniel for DA Lawn Care Solutions. This is an internal business tool, not a public sign-up service.

What the app does

With the operator's Google authorization, the app reads recorded job and client details from selected Google Sheets, generates an invoice JPG using the approved DA invoice design, and uploads it to a configured Google Drive folder.

It checks the uploaded file before writing its link to the selected ledger and marking the invoice CREATED. It includes duplicate prevention and checks that payment status and protected ledger formulas are unchanged.

Current status

Production processing is disabled. Dedicated Google authorization and authenticated acceptance testing remain incomplete. Offline generation and safety tests have passed. This page does not provide access to invoices, client records or credentials.

Google access and control

The existing app requests Google Sheets and Google Drive scopes. These scopes grant broader account access than the particular files selected in the app configuration. The implementation uses configured workbooks and invoice destinations and searches accessible Drive invoice names to prevent duplicates. Authorization can be revoked through the operator's Google Account.

See the privacy policy for data handling and the terms of use for operator responsibilities.
