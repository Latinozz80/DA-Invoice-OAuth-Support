Terms of use

Purpose and permitted use

DA Invoice Automation V4 is a private invoice preparation tool for DA Lawn Care Solutions. Use is limited to the business operator and people explicitly authorized by that operator. It is not a customer portal or a public subscription service.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for the accuracy of recorded jobs, client details and invoice amounts; for authorizing Google access; and for reviewing generated invoices before sending them. A verified upload or CREATED status confirms technical checks, not that the underlying business record is correct.

Production approval

Live invoice processing must remain disabled until the required authenticated acceptance tests pass and the operator authorizes production use. Unattended processing requires a separate approval. The workflow does not automatically send invoices to customers.

Access and data handling

Use only Google and GitHub accounts, files and folders the operator is authorized to access. Keep credentials out of chat, source commits and invoice files. Google data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Review and changes

These terms describe the intended operating rules for this internal tool. They do not create a public purchase offer or subscription.
